# archive/ui: old Ari screens and design mockups

Kept for reference only. Nothing here is served, built or tested. Every file is a single self-contained HTML page:
open it in a browser to see the screen.

All data in these pages is synthetic. The pages were scrubbed when the repo was exported: people, companies,
links and drafted messages were replaced with the invented demo workspace (coined company names such as
Quorvelline, Bravossine or Halvenquist Freight, and clearly fake people such as Maya Sampleton). Any resemblance
to a real company or person is unintended.

| File | Date | What it was |
|---|---|---|
| `ops-v18.12.html` | Oct 17 2026 | The live app (`web/ops.html`) as of v18.12, saved before the v18.13 to v18.16 changes. |
| `ops-v18.12.6.html` | Oct 20 2026 | Backup of the live app at v18.12.6, taken before the simplify pass. |
| `design-taste.html` | Oct 15 2026 | A one-page board of the look and type choices that set the app's design direction. |
| `ops-simplify-mockup.html` | Oct 20 2026 | First mockup of the simplified app: Today, People and focus screens on fake data. |
| `ops-simplify-soft-v2.html` | Oct 20 2026 | Second round of the simplified mockup in the soft light theme, with three accent colours (`?accent=a|b|c`). |
| `bplus-wallet-mockup.html` | Oct 23 2026 | The B+ "wallet" mockup that led to the new app's shell (`app/web/src/b/`). Its built-in snapshot of app data was rebuilt on synthetic data, with the same counts and stages. |

Left out on purpose, because they hold real prospect data that cannot be scrubbed reliably: the Oct 13 to 15
prototypes (records prototypes v1 and v2, the person-page mockup, the dossier page, the first Outbound and Ari
pages and their script) and every screenshot.
