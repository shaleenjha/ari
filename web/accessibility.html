Accessibility

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Our goal

Kyte should be usable by everyone who has access to it, on a phone or a computer.

What we aim for

  • Touch targets at least 48 by 48 pixels.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 against its background.
  • A visible focus outline, and every action reachable with a keyboard.
  • Labels on buttons and icons so screen readers can name them.
  • Layouts that work on small phone screens and with larger text.

The new version of Kyte checks touch target size and contrast automatically before every release.

Known gaps

  • The current app was built before these checks, so some screens may have smaller targets or missing labels.
  • Screen reader support has not been fully tested on every screen.

Report a problem

If something in Kyte is hard to use, email owner@example.com. Tell us the screen, what you tried, and the device and assistive technology you use. We will reply and tell you what we will fix.
