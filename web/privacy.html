Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Ari is a private, invite-only app operated by Owner. This policy explains what Ari does with your data.

What we access

If you choose to connect Gmail, Ari requests the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. Ari looks only at notification emails sent by LinkedIn (for example, "accepted your invitation" or "sent you a message").

What we store

From those notifications Ari stores only parsed fields: the contact's name, the event type, the time of the event, and the Gmail message ID (to avoid processing the same email twice). Ari does not store email bodies, attachments, or any other emails. Ari never sends, deletes, or modifies email.

How we use it

The parsed fields are used only to update your own follow-up list inside Ari. Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Ari's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Data is stored in an encrypted, access-controlled database. OAuth tokens are kept server-side and never on your device. Access is limited to approved accounts.

Your choices

You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Ari Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your data, disconnect and email ops@example.com; it is removed within 30 days.
