Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

Ari is an invite-only app that helps sales reps run their outreach. This policy explains what information Ari collects when you use it, how that information is used, who processes it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. In this policy, "you" means the person using Ari.

Information we collect

Information you provide. The outreach list you keep in Ari and any notes you add to it, and the drafts Ari prepares for you.

Gmail, if you connect it. Ari requests the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly and reads only notification emails sent by LinkedIn (for example, "accepted your invitation" or "sent you a message"). From each of these emails Ari stores the sender's name, their LinkedIn profile link, the type of event (an accepted invitation or a message), the time of the event, and the Gmail message ID, so the same email is not processed twice.

When a LinkedIn email includes the text of a reply or InMail, Ari also stores that text (up to 2,000 characters), the sender's headline (up to 300 characters), and a link to the conversation, so you can answer it. This can include people who are not on your outreach list.

Ari does not store attachments or any email that is not from LinkedIn. Ari never sends, deletes, or changes your email.

How we use information

Ari uses this information only to keep your outreach list and drafts current inside Ari. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Ari's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Service providers

Ari relies on a small number of service providers that process information on its behalf and for no other purpose:

  • Supabase: database, file storage, and backups.
  • Vercel: hosting for the app.
  • Google: Gmail (read only), Google Sheets, and Google Drive, where encrypted backups are kept.
  • Hark: the AI agent that operates Ari. Hark uses third-party AI model providers to draft messages and replies.

Information from Ari may also appear in summaries that you send to your own work email.

Data retention

Ari makes a backup every night and keeps the newest 14. Older backups are deleted automatically, both in storage and in the encrypted copy kept in Google Drive.

Stored records, such as names, events, and reply text, are kept until they are removed. Ari does not delete them automatically today. Temporary records used while you connect your Google account are cleared after 1 day.

Your choices and rights

You can disconnect Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request access to your information or ask for it to be removed, email owner@example.com.

Security

Information is stored with access restricted by role, so each account sees only what it is allowed to see. Backups are encrypted and a copy is kept offsite.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Ari changes. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
