Kyte

Kyte is a private, invite-only workspace that keeps a salesperson's LinkedIn outreach organized: who accepted, who replied, and which follow-ups are due. Kyte prepares; the person does the sending.

Kyte can optionally connect to a user's Gmail with read-only access to notice LinkedIn notification emails (new connections and messages), so the to-do list stays current without manual entry.

Access is limited to approved accounts. Open Kyte.
