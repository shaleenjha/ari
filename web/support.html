Support

Getting help

Kyte is a private, invite-only app run by Owner. For help with anything in Kyte, email owner@example.com. Say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

What Kyte does

  • Keeps your outreach list: who to contact, who accepted, who replied, and which follow-ups are due.
  • Prepares drafts for your messages.
  • If you connect Gmail, reads LinkedIn notification emails (read only) so your list stays current.

What Kyte does not do

  • Kyte never sends a message for you. You do all the sending.
  • Kyte never logs in to LinkedIn or acts on LinkedIn for you.
  • Kyte never sends, deletes, or changes your email.

Deleting your data

To have your information removed from Kyte, email owner@example.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will confirm when it is done. You can also disconnect Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The Privacy Policy explains what is kept and for how long.

FAQ

How do I get access? Kyte is invite only. Ask Owner.

Does Kyte send messages on its own? No. Kyte prepares, you send.

Do I have to connect Gmail? No. Without it, you mark accepts and replies yourself.

Can I use Kyte on my phone? Yes. Open Kyte in your phone browser and add it to your home screen.

Something looks wrong. What do I do? Email owner@example.com and describe it. Nothing in Kyte is sent anywhere until you send it.
